### 📚 Description
**These collections were formed based on data from well-known automatic text paraphrasing tools**, which ensures their practical relevance. The repository will be a valuable resource for researchers, developers in the field of natural language processing (NLP), linguists, and anyone who wants to automate word replacement and enhance text expressiveness, who works deeply with textual data.

### ✨ Key Benefits
1. **Extensive vocabulary coverage** 
   – Tens of thousands of words and stable expressions.
2. **Relevance** 
   – Regular updates from verified sources.
3. **Bilingualism**   
   – Separate files for RU and EN, integrate into any scripts supporting the format `words1|words2|words3`.
4. **Convenient format**  
   – TXT `Hello|Greetings|Glad to see you` — suitable for Python, PHP, C# and others.
5. **High accuracy** 
   – Rarely used and outdated words have been removed.

### 🛠️ Where it's useful
- 🔍 SEO optimization: generation of unique texts
- 🎓 Educational projects: synonym training tools
- 🤖 Bot development: dynamic rephrasing of responses
- 🖋️ Development of extensions for text editors
